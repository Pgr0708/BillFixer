## App Store Description (Full — 4000 char limit)

```
Your medical bill isn't always what you owe.

Bill Fixer helps you understand your medical bill and Explanation of Benefits (EOB) — then builds you an evidence-backed action plan to dispute errors, request discounts, and access financial assistance.

HERE'S HOW IT WORKS:

📄 SCAN YOUR BILL
Photograph or import your medical bill and EOB. Bill Fixer reads them using on-device AI — your documents never leave your phone for processing.

🔍 GET YOUR ANALYSIS
Bill Fixer checks the math, compares your bill with your EOB, looks up your hospital's published prices, and checks if any consumer protection rules apply to your situation.

📋 REVIEW YOUR FINDINGS
See exactly what looks off — with evidence. Every finding shows where the discrepancy comes from: your bill, your EOB, or your hospital's own published pricing data.

✉️ TAKE ACTION
Generate a professional dispute letter, financial assistance application, or phone script — tailored to your specific situation. Ready to send.

📁 TRACK YOUR CASE
Cases take time. Bill Fixer tracks your deadlines, follow-ups, and outcome — so you know exactly where you stand.

WHAT BILL FIXER CHECKS:
• Does your bill match your Explanation of Benefits?
• Are there duplicate charges?
• Does the math add up?
• How does your bill compare to your hospital's published prices?
• Could you qualify for financial assistance or charity care?
• Do the No Surprises Act or Good Faith Estimate dispute rules apply?

WHAT YOU GET:
• Professional dispute letters (tailored to your case)
• Financial assistance application letters
• Phone scripts with real responses to common pushbacks
• A complete evidence pack PDF
• Deadline tracking and reminders

YOUR PRIVACY COMES FIRST:
• Bill reading happens entirely on your device
• Your documents are encrypted and auto-deleted when you're done
• We never sell your data or share it with providers or insurers
```
