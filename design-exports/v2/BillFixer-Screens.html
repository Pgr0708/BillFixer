EVIDENCE · FROM YOUR DOCUMENTS
BILL · PAGE 1 · LINE 3
"03/15   71046   Chest X-Ray 2 Views   420.00"
BILL · PAGE 1 · LINE 7
"03/15   71046   Chest X-Ray 2 Views   420.00"
EOB · CLAIM 8821-X
"71046 — allowed once. Units: 1."
Why this matters

Your insurer allowed one unit. If the second line stays on the bill, you're carrying a charge your plan never approved.

Sometimes a repeat study is legitimate — a follow-up film after a procedure. Ask them to show the second order. If it exists, this closes cleanly.
